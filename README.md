# eranosme 🍲

*The discipline of the shared table: many traditions each lay down a distinct gift, and the gifts are received, recognized, arranged, and served as one coherent whole — every contributor still recognizable.*

eranosme is two roots welded into one word: the Greek **eranos** (ἔρανος), the meal to which every guest brings a dish — the Homeric contributed feast, the classical common-fund where friends pool aid for one in need, and the modern Eranos conferences of Ascona (1933–present) that gathered scholars across traditions to think together — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received* and bestowed as cosmic gift, not made. So eranosme is the contributed-meal carried as an ordinance: the act of hosting a cross-tradition table, received as a given way of gathering, not a host's invention. (The old tie of *eranos* to *erô*, "I love," is debated; eranosme carries the meal-and-host structure the word certainly holds and leaves the love-root a quiet maybe.)

eranosme names a **discipline**, not a single act — the four-beat work of **Receive → Recognize → Arrange → Serve**, by which the Kingdom builds anything genuinely cross-tradition: a forged word, an interfaith council, a comparative seminar, a hospitable potluck. Its light is hosting many gifts into one coherent whole *without conquering any of them* — plurality made coherent without being forced into sameness, and the host's humility built in as a rule, since the gifts come through others' long walking and are gathered, never owned. Its shadow is the two ways the table fails: the host who quietly *takes* instead of hosts, and the synthesis that *dissolves* its donors into grey paste until no dish is recognizable. The first Core forge under POLYPHONIA, forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.85/10**.
